[Atlassian: Understanding incident severity levels](https://www.atlassian.com/incident-management/kpis/severity-levels/) distinguishes incident severity, a measure of business impact, from priority, a measure of urgency. Organisations define their own severity criteria; use the actual task's stated rules and records for any classification.
